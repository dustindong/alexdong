Instructor levels

  • Level 1 - Registered
  • Level 2 - Certified
  • Level 3 - Senior
  • Level 4 - Master

Our level of instructors are based on how long the teachers have been learning and teaching our style. We also look at their contributions to teaching and the spreading of Tai Chi. Last but not least, how many of our forms they practice and teach.
